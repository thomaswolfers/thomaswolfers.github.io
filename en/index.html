Thomas Wolfers

Carl Zeiss Endowed Professor for Artificial Intelligence in Neural Systems Imaging, Friedrich Schiller University Jena

I am fascinated by the complexity of the human mind, in both order and disorder. After a PhD in neuroscience at the Donders Institute in the Netherlands and research positions in Norway and the UK, I now lead the Laboratory for Mental Health Mapping in Jena, Germany.

Science

I focus on individuals rather than diagnostic categories. Using machine learning and large datasets, I aim to understand what shapes mental health and illness, to reduce stigma, and ultimately to improve treatment.

Talks for schools, communities, and organizations

In a world of rapid change, I believe academics have a responsibility to foster public dialogue. I offer pro bono talks and discussions on three topics, and I cover my own travel costs within a two-hour drive of Jena.

Artificial intelligence and machine learningHow AI works, what it can do, and where its limits are.
Brain health, development, and agingHow the brain changes across the lifespan.
Mental healthWhat we know about mental illness, and what we do not.

If you would like to invite me to your school, community, or organization, get in touch. Please include:
