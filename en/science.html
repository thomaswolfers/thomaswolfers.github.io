Science is more than my profession: it is a way to make a real difference in the lives of people facing complex health challenges.

By focusing on individuals rather than categorizing them by diagnosis, I aim to reduce stigma and, ultimately, to improve how we understand and treat the mechanisms underlying suffering. In this work, I apply and develop machine learning methods. It takes place in the Laboratory for Mental Health Mapping and is organized into four connected themes.
